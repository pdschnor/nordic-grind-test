We roast on Tuesdays, in the room behind the bar.

Every bag on our shelf and every shot at the bar comes from the same small drum roaster, a few metres from where you order. We buy green coffee from a handful of farms we know by name and roast it light enough to taste where it came from.

Roasting small means we can change things week to week, and that nothing sits around getting stale.

A week in the life of a bag of beans

  1. Tuesday Roasted in small batches, cooled and bagged by hand the same afternoon.
  2. Wednesday to Sunday Resting. Fresh coffee needs a few days to settle before it tastes its best.
  3. From Monday On the bar and on the shelf, until the next roast comes out.
